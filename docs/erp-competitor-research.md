# SMART ERP — ERP Vendor Market Research (Arab World)

**Goal:** map the companies that sell the _same service_ — ERP systems — in Saudi/MENA, their design and positioning focus, to sharpen SMART ERP's differentiation.

## 1. Market context

- **MENA + Africa ERP market:** $5.38B (2024) → **$10.20B by 2032** (~8.7% CAGR); GCC ERP ≈ $330M; enterprise software MEA $8.7B (2026) → $22.99B (2035).
- **Drivers:** Vision 2030 digital transformation, cloud migration, e-invoicing (ZATCA) mandates, AI/IoT adoption.
- **Global share (est.):** SAP ~22–24%, Oracle ~11–13%, Microsoft Dynamics + others split the rest — but these serve enterprises/gov; the **SMB/mid segment is where regional products win**.

## 3. The direct competitors — design & positioning cards

### دفتـرة Daftra (daftra.com) — the benchmark Arabic SMB ERP

- **Positioning:** "شريكك الرقمي الأمثل… هوية عربية أصيلة وواجهة عصرية" — **Arabic identity + full breadth** (50+ business activities) + **e-invoicing first**.
- **Landing section map:** Hero ("نظام ERP متكامل لإدارة أعمالك" + e-invoice callout + **ابدأ الاستخدام مجانًا** + trust chips: تجربة مجانية · لا حاجة لبطاقة ائتمان · جاهز للعمل فورًا · شامل لجميع التطبيقات) → 50+ مجال → **module accordion blocks, each = module + 3–4 sub-features + "اعرف المزيد" + CTA**: المبيعات/الفواتير → المحاسبة → المخزون/المشتريات → الموارد البشرية → دورة العمل → CRM → التقارير → section breakers with punchy lines ("المحاسبة صارت أسهل حتى لغير المحاسبين!") → **mobile apps** → segmentation (للشركات الكبرى / لرواد الأعمال والشركات الناشئة) → **social proof: +40,000 شركة** + stats (معاملات شهرية، موظفين، أتمتة) → final CTA.
- **Design style:** light, blue/teal, functional; dense but organized; Arabic-first.
- **Takeaways:** module-breadth accordion is the ERP home-page norm; free-trial-no-credit-card chips; "عربية أصيلة" is a real selling point; +40k social proof.

### قيود Qoyod (qoyod.com) — covered in the earlier doc: ZATCA above the fold, 25k companies, two editions (أصحاب أعمال/محاسبون), 14-day trial

### إدارتك Idaratech (idaratech.sa/ar)

- **Positioning:** "أول منصة ERP سعودية حاصلة على اعتماد تقنية سعودية" — **national certification trust**; HR/payroll depth (WPS wage protection, GOSI); claims 500+ companies; AI-powered.
- **Takeaway:** certification/local-compliancce claims (اعتماد سعودي) are a strong differentiator lever; HR/payroll (GOSI/WPS) is a module customers fear — lead with it if we have it.

### زات ERP (zaterp.com) & سند Snad (snad.io) & Envaglo (envaglo.com)

- ZAT: cloud ERP with e-invoicing integration; Snad: Riyadh-founded (2025), integrated modules + POS for SMB; Envaglo: **AI + ZATCA + GOSI** compliance.
- **Takeaway:** new entrants crowd the same message (integrated modules + ZATCA + SMB); compliance + speed-to-value + design quality are the differentiators, not the module list.

### أودو Odoo (Arabic) & SAP Business One (MENA-Arabic)

- Odoo: modular app-store UI, partner-led pricing (~400 SAR/yr entry), dense but clear; SAP B1: enterprise trust, partner-led, less Arabic-native feel.
- **Takeaway:** their design language is global-generic; an Arabic-first, modern, fast landing reads as "local, modern, trustworthy" by contrast.

## 4. What every ERP vendor focuses on (the pattern)

1. **Module breadth as hero** — invoice/accounting/inventory/HR/POS/CRM in accordion blocks; breadth = perceived completeness.
2. **Compliance as trust** — ZATCA e-invoicing, GOSI, WPS, "اعتماد سعودي" badges above the fold and in pricing.
3. **Zero-friction trial** — free, no credit card, "جاهز فورًا", 14-day trial, SAR pricing.
4. **Social proof stats** — +40,000 شركة / 25,000 منشأة / 500+ عميل.
5. **Segmentation** — للشركات الكبرى vs رواد الأعمال/الناشئة; أصحاب أعمال vs محاسبون.
6. **Arabic identity** — "هوية عربية أصيلة", Arabic-first with EN parity, dialect-aware copy.
7. **Mobile apps + offline** — always-present section.
8. **Weakness they share:** dense, template-like, average design; heavy text; rarely great CWV or a11y; "نظام متكامل" claims run together.

## 5. Positioning implications for SMART ERP

| Axis         | Competitors                                             | SMART ERP angle                                                                  |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Product**  | Module breadth (Daftra/Qoyod)                           | Same breadth, **cleaner UX + ERP + payments in one flow** (register→pay→go live) |
| **Trust**    | ZATCA/GOSI/Saudi-certified claims                       | Our own compliance roadmap + **Saudi-built, Arabic-first, SAR pricing**          |
| **Design**   | Dense, dated, template-like                             | **Modern token-based design, strong CWV, a11y ≥ 95** — the visible difference    |
| **Audience** | SMB/mid                                                 | Same SMB/mid; avoid enterprise (SAP/Oracle) this cycle                           |
| **Price**    | Odoo partners ~400 SAR/yr entry; Qoyod/Wafeq 99–999 SAR | Mid-tier pricing with clear value (data-informed in Phase 1)                     |

**One-line positioning draft:** "نظام ERP سعودي متكامل — محاسبة، مخزون، موارد بشرية، وفواتير إلكترونية معتمدة — بأسعار واضحة وتجربة عصرية، يبدأ من التسجيل حتى التشغيل في خطوات قليلة."
